## The defect

Round 142 fixed the supervisor's ring and merged it (PR #89 → `4a02b15`), but
its PR carried no round card. The convention is that each round's card lands in
that round's own PR — `docs/rounds/140.md` arrived in the round-140 merge
commit, `docs/rounds/141.md` in the round-141 one — and §19.4 says plainly:
**a round that closes without a card is not finished**.

The card is the slice of a round's reasoning that survives the clone: `.pair/`
is git-ignored on purpose, so without the card a stranger reading the
repository sees a diff and no reason for it. §19.3 also asks the machine
receipt to point at the card — the receipt for round 142
(`.pair/RECEIPT-142.md`) had no card to point at.

The card could not be added to the round-142 branch: the sanctioned merge
deletes it, by construction (§19.3), so the repair is a PR of its own.

## The honest limits

- **The card lands one round after the round it documents.** The rule is that
  the card is written at close; this one was written after the close, because
  the close is where the omission became visible. The content is the round
  142 receipt's material, measured on the merged head.
- **The receipt for round 142 still points at a card that did not exist when
  it was written.** The receipt is machine-generated in `.pair/` at the close
  and is not rewritten; the link it should have carried now exists.
- **Reopening is not free.** The reopened round inherited the previous round's
  task line, and the branch of the round being documented was already gone.
  A card written before the merge would have avoided both.
